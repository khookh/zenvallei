About this project

This is my personal V0 project for visualising heat vulnerability in Zennevallei and exploring how it relates to nearby green space and soil sealing, two factors that influence the urban heat-island effect.

For now, the heat indicators come exclusively from the Flemish Government’s Department of Care; for land cover, land use and vegetation, I am experimenting with Copernicus data and working to incorporate more relevant data from Copernicus and other sources.

The project is open to feedback and collaboration through GitHub or email; the frontend was developed extensively with assistance from large language models (LLMs), and the dashboard documents the data, where it comes from and how it is processed and used.
